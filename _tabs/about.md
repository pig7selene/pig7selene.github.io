## About This Blog

This blog is my personal archive of notes, experiments, projects, and ideas. Most posts grow out of courses, papers, implementations, or questions that I am trying to understand more carefully.

The topics range from machine learning and natural language processing to large language models, algorithms, theoretical computer science, mathematics, and competitive programming. My goal is to turn scattered learning materials into clear, durable notes that I can return to in the future—and that may also be useful to other learners.
