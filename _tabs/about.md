## Projects

My latest project is **the-foundation-model-handbook**, a growing collection of structured notes on the foundations of modern foundation models.

The notes are generated and organized with the help of **AI**, while I use the project as a way to build a systematic knowledge base for topics related to foundation models, including:

- Transformer
- Pre-training
- Post-training
- Reinforcement Learning
- RAG and Agents
- Model architecture and systems
- Other foundational knowledge for LLM research and engineering

The goal is to gradually turn scattered learning materials into a coherent and structured handbook that I can continuously refine as I learn more.
